Privacy policy

EFFECTIVE 2 JULY 2026

01 The short version

Sable is a local-first browser. Your browsing data belongs to you and stays on your device. Sable contains no telemetry, no analytics, and no advertising identifiers, and we do not sell or share data with anyone — we never receive it in the first place.

02 Data stored on your device

  • History, bookmarks, downloads, and settings are stored in a local database on your computer.
  • Private tabs keep no history and their session data is discarded when the app closes.
  • The ad-blocker's allow-list and daily blocking statistics are stored locally.
  • You can clear browsing history at any time from Settings.

03 Ad & tracker blocking

Sable blocks advertising and tracking requests on-device using the community-maintained EasyList and EasyPrivacy filter lists. Filter lists are downloaded from their public sources and cached locally; no information about the pages you visit is sent anywhere as part of blocking.

04 Network requests Sable makes

Sable itself only contacts the network to (a) load the pages you ask for, (b) fetch filter-list updates, and (c) check for app updates when that feature ships. Your searches go directly to the search engine you chose in Settings.

05 Optional account & sync (upcoming)

A future version will offer optional sign-in with Google to sync bookmarks, history, and settings between your devices. If you enable it: sync data will be transmitted over encrypted connections and stored only to provide the sync service; authentication tokens are kept in your operating system's keychain; and Sable will request only basic profile information (name, email) from Google. Sync will always be opt-in — the browser remains fully usable without an account.
