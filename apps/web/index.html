Sable.

A fast, minimal, privacy-first browser for people who want speed and less clutter.

Download for macOS & Windows Free beta · unsigned builds: macOS right-click → Open, Windows "More info → Run anyway"
01

Ads & trackers blocked, built in

EasyList + EasyPrivacy filtering on every tab — no extensions to install. A live dashboard shows requests blocked, data saved, and a blocked-over-time heatmap.

02

Light on memory, by design

Background tabs suspend automatically after ten minutes — their processes are freed entirely and restored the instant you return. Your laptop's fans will notice.

03

Split view

Two pages side by side in one window with ⌘\ — docs next to the editor, video next to notes. No window juggling.

04

Keyboard-first

A command palette on ⌘K drives everything — search history, switch tabs, toggle blocking, split, suspend. Focus mode on ⌘⇧F hides every pixel of chrome.

05

Private by default

Local-first: history, bookmarks, and settings live in a database on your machine. No telemetry, no analytics, nothing phoned home.
